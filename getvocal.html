GetVocal AI™

A command center for fleets of governed AI agents — designed, systematized, and shipped as a working product surface.

GetVocal is an enterprise AI agent platform running high-stakes conversations for companies like Vodafone, Deutsche Telekom, and Glovo. Its differentiator is architectural: deterministic business logic and generative language, where neither can override the other. V3 needed a product surface that made that governance visible — not a chatbot dashboard, but a control tower.

THE BRIEF

Give operators command of a whole fleet of agents at a glance, then let them drill from fleet to team to a single agent's triggers, operators, and data connections — without the interface ever feeling like a game. Enterprise trust meant precision, auditability, and calm: every path explainable, motion only on state change.

THE WORK

The centerpiece is a living canvas. At fleet zoom, thirty agents arrange themselves through pixel-space physics — teams cluster, handoffs travel as comet arcs, health rolls up into each cluster's ring. Click through and the canvas re-centers on one agent with its constellation of operators, triggers, and data connections fanned around it.

The agent node itself went through a deliberate de-game-ification: from a glowing orb to a quiet capsule holding three co-equal metrics, health carried in a thin ring instead of a bloom. Around it sits a progressive-disclosure language borrowed from OS design — inputs and outputs collapse to single pills, flow is felt through position and hairlines, and panels bloom outward from the node while the rest of the canvas quiets.

Two grounds carry the whole system: a blue-charcoal dark mode where only the rings hold color, and a warm-paper light mode drawn from the Aurora brand book. Sage means healthy, mustard means warning, and coral is reserved for the one moment per frame that genuinely needs you. All of it is tokenized across a Paper design system and shipped as an interactive React prototype the product team uses to test the direction for real.

Golf Guru™

A golf psychology coach that lives in your pocket — brand, product, and design system built as one continuous piece of work.

Golf is played on a six-inch course: the space between your ears. Golf Guru packages sports psychology into audio sessions, mental-game scoring, and pre-round routines for everyday golfers. The brief covered the whole surface — a brand that could sit calmly next to the game's old institutions, and an app that makes working on your head feel as normal as working on your swing.

THE BRAND

The identity starts with the levitating guru: a meditating figure crossed with golf clubs, floating above the ring of a cup. Guru Green does the heavy lifting, supported by Lake Ball teal, Sand Trap cream, and the near-black Twilight 9th — a palette pulled from dusk on a links course rather than a leaderboard. The voice keeps the same calm confidence; the out-of-home line simply says, "It's not your swing."

THE APP

The product organizes a golfer's week around a practice wheel — Prepare, Practice, Play, Pre-Round, Post-Round — with audio sessions from the guru, reflections, and a mental-game score tracked round over round. A full design system carries it: golden-ratio type scale, dials and score charts, call-out cards, and the tracking components that turn soft psychology into hard numbers.

THE REBUILD

In 2026 the app was rebuilt as a working React prototype for this portfolio — three complete flows running in a phone frame you can actually use. The tile in this case study isn't a mockup; it's the product, live. Scroll the wheel, play a session, score a round.
